Custom Notifier tells you before something you use at home runs out, so you can buy more in time. Pills, diapers, coffee, detergent, pet food: anything you consume at a steady pace.

Add a product once and say how fast you use it: how many units, every how many days, and how many units come in a package. Custom Notifier keeps track of the stock for you and works out the day it will run out. You choose how many days before that you want to be alerted.

What you can do
- See all your products in one list, with the stock left, the day each one runs out and the day of its next alert.
- Get one notification before a product runs out, on the day you chose.
- Restock when you buy a new package, or adjust the stock when the count is off.
- Edit or delete a product at any time.
- Optionally add each alert to your calendar as an all-day event, in the calendar you choose. This is off until you turn it on in Settings and allow calendar access.
- Use it in English or Spanish, in light or dark theme.

Private by design
- Everything stays on your device. There is no account, no sign-in and no server.
- No ads and no analytics.
- The app has no internet permission.
- Notifications are created on your device.

Custom Notifier is a small, focused tool: it does not scan barcodes, order products or sync between devices.
