# 0009. English development language, Spanish as a translation

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: Accepted

## Context

The project needs one language for code, specs and terms, and the app needs a Spanish version. This ADR is retroactive: it records a decision taken in [SESSION0.md](../SESSION0.md#constraints-and-technical-decisions) (2026-09-22) and does not revisit it.

## Decision

All code, specs and terms are in English. Spanish is added as a translation at the end of the MVP. All project artifacts are in English, with no personal references (always "the user").

## Consequences

- Spanish translation is the closing step of Phase 1.
